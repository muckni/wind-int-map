## Germany offshore public-data feasibility

- `MaStR` is suitable for public asset and operator registry matching, not for generation time series.
- `SMARD` exposes offshore wind generation at control-zone level with offshore coverage in `50Hertz` and `TenneT`, not at farm or OSS level.
- `Bundesnetzagentur` offshore tender pages are suitable for support / award provenance, not operations telemetry.

Conclusion:
- Wind-farm generation integration: `no` for now, because the public data located here is not clearly wind-farm level.
- Individual OSS transmission integration: `no` for now, because no stable public OSS-level telemetry source was found.
